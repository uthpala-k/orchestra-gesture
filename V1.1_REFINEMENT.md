# V1.1 refinement — sustain, per-voice volume, final master mix

Changes:
- Real sampled sustaining folk voices refresh while SNAP pinch is held:
  Whistle, Pan Flute, Recorder, Ocarina, Musette.
- Plucked voices such as harp and dulcimer still decay naturally.
- Settings -> Melody now has per-voice Volume (0-150%) beside Reverb.
- Settings -> Sound defaults:
  Orchestra mix = 15%
  Solo mix = 70%
- Orchestra/Solo mix sliders now act as literal final bus output faders.
- Existing stronger real-sample Orchestral Pulse arrangement is retained.
