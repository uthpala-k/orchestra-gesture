# Third-Party Notices


## V1.1 pastoral voices and pulse patterns

The V1.1 Tin Whistle, Low Whistle, Pan Flute, Recorder, Celtic Harp,
Hammered Dulcimer, Mandolin and Musette voice models are generated by the
Orchestra / Gesture application code using Tone.js. No additional third-party
folk sample library is redistributed for these voices.

The V1.1 Orchestral Pulse patterns are original algorithmic patterns generated
at runtime. They do not reproduce or redistribute a film soundtrack recording,
melody, MIDI file, or loop.


## FluidR3_GM sampled folk/pastoral instruments

V1.1 uses a sparse browser-ready pitch subset of FluidR3_GM obtained from:

https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts

The Fluid soundfont samples are distributed under Creative Commons
Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0). Keep `public/folk-samples/ATTRIBUTION.txt`
with deployments that include these voices.

The app uses these sample-based voices for Whistle, Pan Flute, Recorder,
Ocarina, Harp, Dulcimer and Accordion/Musette.

## V1.1 orchestral pulse

Orchestral Pulse uses the project's existing VSCO 2 CE / VCSL-compatible
orchestral sample workflow. The importer now creates a pulse-manifest from
short articulations such as staccato, spiccato and pizzicato when available.
No commercial soundtrack loop is redistributed.


## V1.1 attribution reminder

The per-voice sustain, volume, reverb and master-mix controls are project code
and do not change the licenses of the audio libraries.

Deployments containing the processed solo sample bank must retain:
`public/solo-samples/ATTRIBUTION.txt`

Deployments containing the FluidR3_GM folk/pastoral sample bank must retain:
`public/folk-samples/ATTRIBUTION.txt`
